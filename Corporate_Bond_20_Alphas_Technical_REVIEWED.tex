\begin{abstract}Implementation-level specifications for twenty corporate-bond microstructure and relative-value signals targeting five- and ten-trading-day residualised returns. The production object is executable net return conditional on fill, with point-in-time data, state classification, transaction costs and abstention. No signal is claimed to guarantee transaction-cost coverage.\end{abstract}\tableofcontents\newpage
\section{Common target and notation}
Let $i$ index bonds, $g(i)$ issuers, $t$ decision timestamps and $H\in\{5,10\}$ trading days. Let $P^*_{i,t}$ be a causal efficient dirty (or consistently total-return) price. Define the diagnostic residual return
\[y^{mid}_{i,t,H}=R^*_{i,t:H}-\widehat\beta_{i,t}^{\top}\Delta F_{t:t+H}-CarryRoll^{model}_{i,t,H},\]
where $R^*$ includes coupon/accrual exactly once under the selected return convention. The production target is the signed executable package return
\[y^{exec}_{i,t,H}=R^{bond,exec}_{i,t,H}-R^{hedge,exec}_{i,t,H}-C^{fees}_{i,t,H}-C^{fin}_{i,t,H}-C^{impact}_{i,t,H}-C^{borrow}_{i,t,H}.\]
All terms use the same return denominator. Define price confidence $c\in[0,1]$, information probability $p^{info}$, pressure-completion probability $p^{complete}$ and fill probability $p^{fill}$. Every changed hedge leg is costed. Historical fields unavailable at decision time are forbidden. Use chronological purged validation, held-out issuers, point-in-time terms/ratings/index membership, realistic quote latency and a daily position ledger. Unless stated otherwise, $s$ denotes OAS or another consistently defined spread in basis points; positive spread residual means cheap in spread space, while positive price residual means rich in price space. Convert spread forecasts to price returns with the local spread-duration sign, $\Delta P/P\approx-SD\,\Delta s$, before combining with price-space signals.\newpage
\section{Signal 01: Leave-one-out issuer-curve cheapness}
\subsection{Mathematics and feature construction}
The canonical raw score is
\begin{equation}z^{iss}_{i,t}=\frac{s^{fresh}_{i,t}-\widehat s^{(-i)}_{g,t}(T_i)-\gamma^\top q_i}{\widehat\sigma_{i,t}+\epsilon}.\end{equation}
Retain level, one- and three-day change, persistence, acceleration, trailing percentile and robust z-score. Add interactions with $c_{i,t}$, $p^{info}_{i,t}$, $p^{complete}_{i,t}$, liquidity regime, volatility and requested size. Thresholds and normalisation are learned on training data only. Missingness is explicit.

\subsection{Point-in-time implementation}
Maintain an immutable as-of ledger with economic, publication and receipt timestamps, corrections/cancels, security-master version and entitlements. At each decision time freeze the universe; resolve terms and corporate actions; construct fresh prices; compute peers/factors from earlier data; calculate the feature; obtain or simulate a fresh executable entry; and persist the feature snapshot. A confidence-weighted price can use
\[w_q=e^{-age_q/\tau_a}\min(1,size_q/v_0)w_d e^{-dispersion_q/\tau_\sigma},\qquad P^*=\frac{\sum_qw_qP_q}{\sum_qw_q}.\]
Never use future prints through symmetric smoothing.

\subsection{Five- and ten-day forecast}
Benchmark each horizon with
\[y^{exec}_{i,t,H}=a_H+b_Hz+d_Hzc+e_Hzp^{info}+f_Hzp^{complete}+u_{i,t,H}.\]
For dislocations estimate $D_{t+h}=D_te^{-\kappa h}+\epsilon$ and expected capture $D_t(1-e^{-\kappa H})$; for information diffusion use a bounded catch-up fraction. This gives an economic mapping from state to the 5d/10d horizon.

\subsection{Execution and costs}
Estimate jointly
\begin{align}
p^{fill}&=\Pp(fill\mid X,size,protocol),\\
C&=g(size,spread,ADV,depth,vol,flow,toxicity,protocol),\\
\mu_H&=\E[y^{exec}_H\mid fill,X].
\end{align}
Use a fresh executable entry, never the historical print that revealed the signal. Model quote withdrawal and adverse selection. Relative-value packages pay every leg. Capacity is evaluated by rerunning net P\&L at increasing requested size and participation constraints.

\subsection{Falsification and monitoring}
Kill the feature if it disappears with fresh independent prices, relies on unavailable data, is concentrated in a few issuers/events, or loses its edge under realistic fills and costs. Run leave-one-issuer-out tests, IG/HY and liquidity splits, calm/stress regimes, protocol splits and alternative residualisations. Monitor drift, price confidence, fill calibration, realised versus predicted costs, mark-outs and residual factor exposure.
\subsection{Signal-specific mechanism and implementation}
Data: Trades + fresh quotes + analytics  |  Target: Base and issuer-neutral  |  5 and 10 days gap\_i = OAS\_i - curve\_issuer,-i(tenor\_i) - liquidity\_adjustment\_i Mechanism and evidence.  A bond can be cheap relative to economically comparable liabilities of the same obligor without the issuer itself being cheap. The research hypothesis is convergence of the bond-specific gap, not compensation for broad credit risk. Liquidity and OTC-friction evidence motivates controlling persistent differences; no cited paper validates this exact spline signal at these horizons. Feature and implementation.  Fit a robust, regularised issuer spread curve using only contemporaneously available peers, excluding the target bond. Weight observations by freshness, executable size and inverse estimated measurement variance. Use a sector/rating/tenor prior when issuer support is thin, but keep the prior uncertainty. As an initial eligibility rule require at least four useful peer observations across maturity, not four stale dealer copies. Control issue size, age, bid--ask cost and contractual differences. Keep legal obligor, guarantee and seniority consistent. Fit spread differences in basis points, retain the raw gap and its standard error, and standardise by a past-only robust residual scale. A positive adjusted gap is a candidate long-price signal. Cap extrapolation beyond the peer maturity range. Five-/ten-day forecast and hedge.  Estimate conditional gap dynamics from previously observed, mature outcomes. Convert the expected narrowing into price-return basis points using spread duration; fit separate five- and ten-day coefficients. A ten-day position is not automatically twice a five-day position. Prefer an issuer-neutral label and hedge against liquid sibling bonds; remove remaining Treasury key-rate exposures separately. Condition expected convergence on a recent technical dislocation or improving flow. A static wide spread with no demonstrated short-horizon decay is not sufficient. Executable entry and costs.  Compute the gap again at the actually available ask for a purchase and at the hedge bonds’ bids, with valid size and timestamps. Admit only after the common all-leg cost and uncertainty gate. Exit at the chosen horizon, or earlier under a pretested convergence/invalidation policy. Use a no-trade band to avoid repeatedly crossing for tiny model changes. Falsification / kill test.  Reject gaps explained by a stale target quote, different recovery claim, embedded option, tender, tax treatment or genuine liquidity premium. Refit without the single largest-weight peer. Kill the model if out-of-sample incremental net performance disappears against the near-twin signal or when both endpoints require fresh executable observations. A fair-value model can be precise and still wrong about convergence speed.
\subsection{Agent implementation runbook: data discovery to safe production}
\textbf{Operating rule.} The agent must advance through auditable gates rather than implement the entire alpha in one pass. If a required field is unavailable, ambiguous, delayed, or not historically point-in-time, stop that branch and record the limitation; never silently substitute a convenient proxy.
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em,label=\textbf{A\arabic*.}]
\item \textbf{Data discovery.} Inventory candidate sources/tables/files; definitions; keys; units; time zones; economic, publication and receipt timestamps; corrections/cancels; historical coverage; entitlements; delayed-history rules; and whether each field was genuinely observable live. Output schema and coverage diagnostics only.
\item \textbf{Raw profiling.} Report rows, bonds, issuers, days, missingness, duplicates, timestamp inversions, corrections, size distributions and join success by IG/HY, liquidity, sector and period. Do not impute yet.
\item \textbf{Canonical identifiers.} Version the security master and map security IDs to legal obligor, parent, guarantee, seniority, currency, coupon, maturity, option schedule and corporate actions. Quarantine ambiguous/time-varying mappings.
\item \textbf{As-of layer.} Require every query to accept a decision timestamp and return only records then available. Unit-test late records, corrections and cancels. Persist available-at and provenance fields.
\item \textbf{Price layer.} Normalize clean/dirty price, accrued, spread convention, side, size and quote status. Create freshness, executable-size, dispersion and dealer-breadth metadata. Any forward fill must explicitly age and lose confidence.
\item \textbf{Analytics layer.} Produce cash flows, yield/OAS as appropriate, spread duration, convexity, KRD, CS01/DV01, carry and roll from point-in-time terms; reconcile a sample against an independent implementation.
\item \textbf{Targets.} Build 5d/10d efficient-price diagnostic and executable-return labels. Estimate residualisation only on prior data; cost every changed hedge leg, fees, financing and impact. Persist label maturity for purging.
\item \textbf{Signal-specific prerequisites.} Execute the following in order, emitting exclusions, distributions and hand-audited examples after each sub-step:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*)]
\item Discover issuer hierarchy, guarantee/seniority, OAS analytics, terms, trades and executable quotes.
\item Build same-economic-claim sibling eligibility and point-in-time peer sets.
\item Construct confidence-weighted fresh target/peer spreads; require adequate maturity support.
\item Fit leave-one-out issuer curve, add contractual/liquidity adjustments and retain prediction uncertainty.
\item Recompute cheapness at target executable side and hedge executable sides before admission.
\item Refit without largest-weight peer and reject extrapolation/stale-price-driven gaps.
\end{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Literal raw feature.} Translate the documented equation exactly. Assert units, sign, finite values, peer counts, timestamp ordering and boundaries. Hand-reconcile at least 20 events.
\item \textbf{Causal transforms.} Add rolling median/MAD z-score, changes, persistence, acceleration and percentile with past-only windows. Fit curves/betas/normalizers only inside training windows and version them.
\item \textbf{State variables.} Join price confidence, information/toxicity, pressure completion, liquidity regime, volatility, event age and requested size as separate columns before tuning interactions.
\item \textbf{Leakage tests.} Append records arriving after decision time and prove historical feature values do not change. Compare live-available with enhanced-history fields and test shifted timestamps. Any failure blocks modelling.
\item \textbf{Measurement tests.} Recompute with fresh quotes only, trades only, independent prices where possible, alternative stale thresholds and stricter confidence. Reject effects caused only by asynchronous updating.
\item \textbf{Transparent baseline.} Fit horizon-specific linear/robust models in walk-forward folds before nonlinear models; record sign, stability, sample size and incremental information.
\item \textbf{State/convergence models.} Estimate information versus temporary-pressure probabilities and convergence speed where meaningful. Cross-fit state probabilities used as features.
\item \textbf{Execution simulator.} Request a new executable entry after the signal timestamp; apply latency, size, withdrawal, partial-fill and legging rules. No executable observation means unfilled, not a midpoint fill.
\item \textbf{Fill/cost models.} Estimate fill probability and conditional spread/impact/fees/financing by size, protocol and state. Calibrate on held-out fills and cost every changed leg.
\item \textbf{Net outcomes.} Store expected executable return conditional on fill, conservative lower quantile and capacity. Keep gross, mark, executable and fully-net outcomes as distinct audit columns.
\item \textbf{Adversarial validation.} Use chronological purged folds, issuer holdouts, liquidity/rating/regime splits, event exclusions, alternative residualisations, placebo dates and within-family ablations.
\item \textbf{Shadow mode.} Freeze versions and generate live predictions without trading. Log source timestamps, features, quotes, hypothetical orders, predicted fills/costs and 1d/5d/10d mark-outs.
\item \textbf{Canary.} Start with strict size, concentration, liquidity and turnover limits. Kill automatically on timestamp anomalies, data gaps, feature drift, cost underprediction, unexplained residual beta or repeated adverse selection.
\item \textbf{Promotion/rollback.} Promote only a fully reproducible version. Default to no-trade when health checks fail and retain one-command rollback to the prior approved model.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em,label=\textbf{P\arabic*.}]
\item ``Inventory only the required data, schemas, keys, timestamps, live-history availability, entitlements, coverage and unresolved questions. Do not build a feature.''
\item ``Build and unit-test canonical identifiers and as-of joins. Return diagnostics and quarantined ambiguities. Do not calculate alpha.''
\item ``Build the price/analytics layer with freshness/confidence and reconcile a hand-audited sample. Stop on unexplained discrepancies.''
\item ``Implement only the literal raw signal with assertions and 20 hand-reconciled examples. Do not optimize parameters.''
\item ``Add past-only transforms and state variables. Run leakage and measurement-artifact tests before fitting any predictive model.''
\item ``Fit the transparent walk-forward baseline for 5d/10d. Report coefficients, uncertainty, ablations and failures; do not tune on the final test period.''
\item ``Add execution, fill and transaction-cost simulation. Recompute conditional executable net outcomes and mark unavailable opportunities unfilled.''
\item ``Run adversarial validation and within-family ablations. Produce a go/no-go research recommendation based only on predeclared gates.''
\item ``Run shadow mode with frozen versions. Do not send orders. Compare predicted versus observable fills, costs and mark-outs.''
\item ``Only after explicit human approval, create a tightly limited canary configuration with hard risk limits, health checks and rollback; do not broaden limits automatically.''
\end{enumerate}

Data: Synchronous sibling quotes + cash-flow risk  |  Target: Issuer-neutral preferred  |  5 and 10 days kink = s\_middle - [w*s\_short + (1-w)*s\_long]; then remove signal 01 Mechanism and evidence.  This isolates a local distortion in curve shape rather than an overall cheap bond or broad issuer spread move. Its distinct hypothesis is that a temporary concentration of demand at one maturity creates curvature that subsequently relaxes. This is a proposed implementation based on relative pricing; OTC-friction research supplies background, not a proven butterfly alpha. Feature and implementation.  Choose short-, middle- and long-maturity bonds with the same economic credit claim and reliable current quotes. Interpolate the two wings to the middle bond’s tenor to construct a spread-space curvature feature; w is the interpolation weight, not the trading notional. Remove the broad leave-one-out value score and persistent local curve shape using training-only regressions. A positive remaining kink suggests the middle bond is cheap. Record wing width, coupon mismatch, liquidity asymmetry, the age of all quotes and the condition number of the hedge system. Start with adjacent liquid maturities; do not optimise thousands of arbitrary triplets on the test set. Five-/ten-day forecast and hedge.  Fit five- and ten-day mean curvature changes separately, conditioned on a transient-flow trigger and issuer event calendar. Estimate results against the issuer-neutral target. Size the tradable package with cash-flow/key-rate and spread-sensitivity calculations, not equal face values. A three-bond package cannot generally neutralise every Treasury key rate, credit level, credit slope and cash constraint simultaneously: add Treasury hedges or relax a documented constraint. Record the resulting residual slope/convexity risk. Executable entry and costs.  Evaluate all three bonds and any rate hedges at contemporaneous executable prices. Count two-way costs on every changed leg. A small apparent curvature edge is particularly vulnerable to multi-leg friction and legging risk. Set a maximum time between leg executions, reserve for failed hedges, and cancel the package when one required leg is not available. Prefer rebalancing existing inventory to opening a completely new butterfly. Falsification / kill test.  Exclude maturity points with real structural segmentation, unusual call schedules, concentrated redemptions or covenant/recovery differences. Run leave-one-wing-out stability tests and compare with a simpler target-versus-one-sibling trade. This is low priority because much of its information may already be in signal 01 while costs are higher. Retain it only if its net contribution survives that direct ablation.

Data: Bond master + paired trades / quotes  |  Target: Issuer-neutral preferred  |  5 and 10 days pair\_gap = (s\_i - s\_j) - expected\_persistent\_spread\_difference(i,j) Mechanism and evidence.  Two close substitutes can temporarily diverge, but a persistent liquidity or contractual difference is not mispricing. This signal tests the unexpected change in their relationship, not whether spreads should be identical. Corporate-bond illiquidity evidence motivates the adjustment. The exact matching, decay and trading rule are research proposals. Feature and implementation.  Build a point-in-time nearest-neighbour graph within legal obligor, currency, seniority and guarantee. Match on effective maturity, cash-flow shape, coupon, issue age, size and trading activity; explicitly exclude optionality or structural mismatches. Require current prices for both members. Estimate their normal spread difference with a robust trailing model using, for example, 60--120 available trading sessions, while preserving uncertainty for young bonds. subject to issuer, sector, rating, duration, CS01, liquidity, turnover, locate and concentration limits. Rebalance only when expected improvement exceeds the cost of changing the existing book.

\section{Extending each signal into a family}
A scalar feature should become a causal family
\[\mathcal F(x)=\{x,\Delta_1x,\Delta_3x,persistence,acceleration,rank,age,x\times c,x\times p^{info},x\times p^{complete}\}.\]
For relative-value signals create IG/HY, sector, seniority, currency, tenor, issue-size, liquidity and callable/non-callable variants with hierarchical shrinkage. For information diffusion vary leader source, innovation window, confirmation breadth and event class. For flow/pressure signals vary side, size bucket, parent-order persistence, completion definition and execution protocol. For institutional mechanics vary event stage, expected mechanical flow, crowding and time-to-completion.

Family expansion must not become feature mining. Pre-register transformations, cap effective degrees of freedom, cluster highly correlated variants, and require incremental value at the family level before inspecting individual members. A family is useful when it generalises a mechanism across bonds; it is dangerous when it merely creates hundreds of correlated thresholds around the same historical episodes.

\subsection{Hierarchical representation}
Let $x_{i,t,k}$ be variant $k$ of a family. A partial-pooling benchmark is
\[y_{i,t,H}=\alpha_H+\sum_k(\bar\beta_{H,f}+u_{H,k})x_{i,t,k}+\epsilon,\qquad u_{H,k}\sim N(0,\tau_f^2).\]
In trees, approximate this with shared base features plus categorical bucket interactions and strong regularisation. Family summaries should be cross-fitted so the downstream combiner never sees an in-sample fitted value.

\section{How the combined model should be fitted}
Use nested walk-forward estimation. First define an untouched final test period. Within the remaining history, create rolling outer folds. In each outer training sample: rebuild the point-in-time universe; fit efficient-price and residualisation models; construct peers and leader graphs; fit state/fill/cost models; generate cross-fitted engine summaries; tune the final return/quantile model on an inner validation window; freeze all parameters; then score the outer validation window. Concatenate only out-of-fold predictions to choose the trading gate and portfolio hyperparameters.

The final model is refit once on all pre-test data using frozen hyperparameter rules and evaluated once on the untouched test. Report results by 5d/10d horizon, IG/HY, liquidity, size, protocol, issuer holdout, event/non-event state and capacity. The primary metric is conservative executable net return conditional on fill with turnover/capacity; secondary metrics include rank IC, calibration, hit rate, drawdown and residual exposures. A production candidate must remain useful after realistic latency, costs, failed RFQs and quote withdrawals.
