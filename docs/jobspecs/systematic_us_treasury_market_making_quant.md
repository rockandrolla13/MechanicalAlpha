# Quantitative Researcher / Trader — Systematic Fixed Income Market Making

**Level:** Experienced

**Location:** [New York]

**Department:** Trading, Research and Machine Learning

We are building a systematic fixed income market-making business focused on US Treasury cash–futures basis, off-the-run Treasuries and rates ETFs. We are looking for a quantitative researcher and trader who can turn a deep understanding of these markets into better prices, smarter hedges and stronger trading decisions.

You will work directly with traders and engineers, using market data to develop models, test them against real trading costs and bring them into production. This is a role for someone who wants to own the full trading problem: from finding an opportunity to understanding how the strategy performs when markets move.

## What you'll do

- Develop systematic pricing and quoting strategies for Treasury basis, off-the-run bonds and rates ETFs. Improve how our prices respond to market moves, liquidity, inventory and incoming flow.
- Price the Treasury cash–futures basis across the deliverable basket. Model conversion factors, cheapest-to-deliver selection, implied repo, carry, funding, delivery and hedge ratios. Understand how repo specialness and a cheapest-to-deliver switch change the trade.
- Build fair-value models for off-the-run Treasuries using the curve, nearby bonds, benchmark issues and futures. Account for issue-specific liquidity, financing and the cost of exiting a position.
- Develop live valuations and hedges for rates ETFs using their underlying bonds, creation and redemption baskets where relevant, and related Treasury instruments. Identify when an apparent ETF premium or discount reflects a tradable opportunity or stale bond prices.
- Research relative-value opportunities across cash bonds, futures and ETFs. Test ideas with realistic assumptions for execution, funding, market impact and risk before deploying them.
- Improve inventory management and automated hedging across the desk. Monitor positions using DV01, key-rate exposures and other risks that matter to each trade.
- Analyse live quotes, fills and P&L to find where our models work and where they need to improve. Respond quickly when market conditions or model behaviour change.
- Partner with traders and engineers to take research into production and help shape the desk's next trading capabilities.

## Who you are

- You have hands-on experience pricing and risk-managing US Treasury cash–futures basis. You can explain the economics of the trade, including funding and delivery, and know where a seemingly attractive basis can lose money.
- You have worked in electronic market making or systematic trading in Treasuries, rates ETFs or a closely related fixed income market. Experience with off-the-run Treasury pricing is particularly valuable.
- You are strong in fixed income analytics, statistical research and programming. You use Python or similar tools to work with market data and can collaborate on production trading systems.
- You think in terms of executable prices and realised results. You account for spreads, fills, hedging costs, financing and changing liquidity in your research.
- You are comfortable making decisions with incomplete information, testing your assumptions and changing course when the data disagrees.
- You communicate clearly and enjoy solving trading problems with researchers, traders and engineers.

Experience in investment-grade corporate bonds, credit ETFs or portfolio trading would be useful as the business expands. It is not required for this role today.

## What you'll get

The opportunity to help build a systematic fixed income desk and see your ideas tested in live markets. You will work closely with people across trading, research and engineering, with direct ownership of models and decisions that affect the desk's performance.
